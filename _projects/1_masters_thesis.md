For my Master's thesis in the [Learning & Adaptive Systems Group](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/) at ETH Zürich, supervised by Frederike Lübeck and Andreas Krause, I work on reinforcement learning for LLM agents that act over many turns and use tools. The focus is on self-distillation and continual learning for multi-turn agentic tasks.

Code and a write-up will be linked here once they are public.
