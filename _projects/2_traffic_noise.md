During a research internship in the [Laboratory for Acoustics / Noise Control](https://www.empa.ch/web/s509) at Empa, I worked on predicting noise pollution from drone footage. Recording video with a drone is much easier than setting up acoustic measurement equipment, so being able to estimate the noise profile of a road from video alone is of great practical benefit.

I developed a Python pipeline that detects and tracks vehicles in the drone footage using optical flow and produces their velocity and acceleration profiles, which serve as input for the noise predictions.
