As an AI engineering intern at the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR) in Bangkok, I was the project manager leading the design and development of DATUM, a retrieval-augmented LLM system for large-scale knowledge access.

- Designed a Python ingestion and preprocessing pipeline, including document chunking strategies.
- Experimented with retrieval configurations to improve relevance and factual grounding.
- Implemented embedding and vector search with the OpenAI API, PostgreSQL and Azure AI Search.
- Built a chatbot prototype for internal evaluation and deployment.

I wrote about the internship and what I learned there in [The Human Aspect of Artificial Intelligence](https://ethambassadors.ethz.ch/2026/08/06/the-human-aspect-of-artificial-intelligence/) on the ETH Ambassadors blog.
