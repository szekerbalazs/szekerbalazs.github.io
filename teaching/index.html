Teaching

I enjoy teaching, and I have done a fair amount of it alongside my studies: as a teaching assistant at ETH Zürich, as a private tutor, and through the course summaries I write and share.

Teaching Assistant at ETH Zürich

Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence (Autumn Semester)

Master’s course by Andreas Krause. Course websites: autumn 2026, autumn 2025.

  • Teaching tutorials. Recorded (ETH login required):
    • Gaussian Processes (2026): from linear and Bayesian linear regression to Gaussian processes, kernels, inference and sampling, followed by old exam questions
  • Creating homework
  • Creating and revising the coding assignments as part of the projects team

Introduction to Machine Learning (Spring Semester)

Bachelor’s course by Andreas Krause and Fanny Yang. Course websites: spring 2026, spring 2025.

  • Teaching tutorials. Recorded (ETH login required):
    • Generative Models and Gaussian Mixture Models (2026): Gaussian Bayes classifiers with an interactive demo of LDA, QDA and naive Bayes, Gaussian mixture models, the EM algorithm and anomaly detection
    • Neural Networks (2025): from linear regression to neural networks, training and activation functions, followed by old exam questions
    • Neural Networks Homework (2025): constructing ReLU networks, gradient descent, and parameter counts of MLPs versus CNNs
  • Creating and reviewing homework

Private Tutoring

  • EduQuant: one-to-one lessons for ETH courses in machine learning, statistics and mathematics, including Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence, Introduction to Machine Learning and Analysis.
  • Simple Learning: probability and statistics, analysis and calculus, linear algebra, and differential equations.
